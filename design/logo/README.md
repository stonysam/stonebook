# Stonebook logo

Stone cottage line drawing, red roof, chimney, one heart-shaped stone (version A "classic", chosen by Crystal 20 Oct 2026; B "bold" was used briefly).

- `cottage.py` draws the variants (`python3 cottage.py <outdir>`); `cottage-a-classic.svg` is the chosen one; `cottage-b-bold.svg` is the bolder alternative.
- `icon.svg`, `maskable.svg`, `apple-touch.svg`, `badge.svg` are the sources for `public/icons/*.png` and `app/icon.png`.
